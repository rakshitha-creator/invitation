# Asset credits — Sita & Ram wedding invitation

## Pinterest reference media (`references/`, `public/ref/`)

The opening video, the hero and every scene illustration come from the ten Pinterest pins the
couple chose (full list with URLs in **REFERENCES.md**). They were only cut into layers,
cropped or re-encoded. These works belong to their creators — several are commercial invitation
templates — so buy or get permission for them (especially the two videos, refs 1 and 10) before
sharing the site publicly.

## Other assets
Temporary wedding photos are from Unsplash (listed below). Gold divider, icons and music were made
for this project in code. No 3D models are used.
